# Council

Challenge the user's requested subject: an analysis, explanation, diagnosis, estimate, decision, proposal, design or implementation. An existing solution is not required. Establish which conclusion would guide action and which objections could materially change it.

## Reviewers

Choose the smallest capable team through Team Up's Arena selection step. One fresh reviewer may be enough for a bounded question. Additional reviewers should contribute independent perspectives or cover distinct consequential uncertainties; neither three seats nor three providers is a requirement. Honor explicit counts and named members, and resolve unavailable explicit choices under Team Up's selection policy. Report actual participants and independence gaps.

Give reviewers the request and relevant context. Keep the initial reviews independent and product code read-only. Include competing evidence and relevant constraints, including known limitations of the assessment. Reviewers may challenge framing, missing alternatives, estimates, trade-offs and evidence quality as well as executable behavior.

## Evidence and synthesis

Keep these distinctions visible:

- **Reproduced failure:** a test or probe demonstrates a violation; include the command and outcome, distinguishing the reviewer's run from the lead's reproduction.
- **Supported objection:** cited evidence and explicit reasoning expose a material problem without an executable demonstration.
- **Unresolved assumption:** a consequential possibility remains unsettled; name the missing evidence and the cheapest useful check.

Summarize useful findings, material disagreements and recommended next steps. Explain which parts of the original assessment survive. Clear or dismiss objections contradicted by the evidence or outside the agreed constraints; keep the reason. Reviewer votes and numeric scores do not establish correctness.

Use [Doubt discipline](doubt.md) to reconcile findings and bound follow-up. A changed assessment can receive focused fresh review when necessary. Agreement is not a requirement to stop, and a challenge does not authorize automatic fixes, PR edits or tracker updates. Deliver the assessment to the caller with the evidence and uncertainty needed for its next decision.
