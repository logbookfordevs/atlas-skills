## Which polish and writing methods add capability beyond Atlas?

### Takeaway

Keep Writing for Humans as the human-writing owner. Technical Writing contains a useful technical-documentation branch; Unslop is an alternative anti-pattern catalog rather than a second mandatory prose pass. No Comments has a concrete destructive policy conflict with Atlas's enduring-invariant comments, so its existing body should not become Atlas's routine cleanup default.

### Cited Findings

**Provenance and scope.** The supplied Pstack repository is a standalone mirror of `cursor/plugins/pstack`, with harness-neutral edits. It identifies Lauren Tan as plugin author and carries an MIT notice naming her. That establishes the declared distribution provenance, not original authorship of every sentence. The mirror expressly says that it rewrites some skills, and its README's dependency summary is incomplete: it calls Show Me Your Work and Automate Me standalone even though their bodies call Unslop, and the latter calls authoring tooling and reads Poteto Mode. Preserve both the upstream Cursor lineage and exact mirror revision in any source receipt. ### Inferences

- Highest value writing change is a technical-document branch with explicit factual-preservation priority, not accumulating several passes that fight over punctuation. A short excerpt or reference can be integrated with provenance; adopting the full workflow requires closing its Unslop dependency.
- A deliberately invoked native `no-comments` can be useful for dead narration and commented-out code, but the original workflow is an implementation/refactor executor, not a read-only style audit. Any report should distinguish those actions.

### Gaps

- No model trials establish that Unslop beats the shipped Writing for Humans plus Stop Slop tree on Leonardo's technical prose. Use representative prose with necessary qualifiers and actual domain terminology before replacing a method.
- Original authorship beyond Pstack's declared author/license is not proven. Do not describe these files as invented by the mirror owner or as copied from Stop Slop without lineage evidence.

## How do learning, audit trails, and personalization compose with Atlas?

`correct` is a small, useful execution complement to Matt's advisory `retro`. `show-me-your-work` adds decision provenance, but its mandatory transcript and cross-model review make it considerably heavier than its TSV format suggests. `automate-me` has useful consent and workspace boundaries, yet needs host-aware authoring and landing policy before recommendation.
