# Polish, learning, and Bro: composition options after router review

Analysis date: 2026-10-15. This note revises packaging judgments without changing the previous note or any runtime package. Pins: Pstack `1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085`; Matt `49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336`; Atlas `1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d`; Bro `523ed36584ec877b975db8c418ef12d485235d0e`. “Preferred” below means a proposed topology if adopting the capability, not a verified implementation or demonstrated output-quality improvement. Source-contract facts and packaging judgments are separated.

## What belongs inside the existing writing and code routers?

### Takeaway

Technical Writing belongs naturally inside Writing for Humans; Unslop can replace or serve as an alternative to its anti-pattern source without a new entry point. No Comments can technically be adapted into Review/Simplify, but those adaptations remove much of its defining policy and duplicate existing behavior. Prefer not to adopt that full workflow unless a trial demonstrates a distinct benefit.

### Cited Findings

**Source-contract facts.** Writing for Humans already routes by genre, audience, author contract and requested intervention, bundles Stop Slop, and excludes agent instructions. It requires supported facts, intended meaning and authorial voice to survive. Technical Writing contributes specific documentation modes and sentence-disambiguation checks, excludes product UI, and calls Unslop on every touched document. Its own rule allows exceptions when compliance worsens a sentence. Unslop explicitly preserves meaning/tone and permits justified passive voice; Stop Slop's carried entry bans passive constructions and all adverbs, its structures discourage three-item groups, and one example removes a “most” qualifier. These are real source conflicts, not a reason to forbid composition. [Writing router L9-26](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/writing-for-humans/SKILL.md#L9-L26), [Technical Writing L9-96](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/technical-writing/SKILL.md#L9-L96), [Unslop L9-62](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/unslop/SKILL.md#L9-L62), [Stop Slop tree and pin](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/sources/composition.json#L510-L551), [Stop Slop example L15-23](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/blob/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9/references/examples.md#L15-L23).

**Writing topology judgment.**

| Candidate | Preferred | Plausible alternative | Rejected/weak placement and reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical Writing | **Adopt as a conditional documentation method inside Writing for Humans.** Trigger for tutorials/how-tos/reference/explanations, README/RFC work and explicit Technical Writing requests. Preserve the reader/author contract; select a document mode and check commands/examples, then apply one chosen prose catalog. Keep prose truth and genre above blanket punctuation, word-count, table and opinion bans. | Native `technical-writing` remains useful for consumers who deliberately want only this technical standard. A shared technical-doc reference can be composed into another documentation owner without exposing another skill. | Replacing all Writing for Humans with Technical Writing loses article/narrative/voice and reader-journey guidance. Putting it into Writing for Agents conflates human documentation and executable instructions. A new Atlas writing router duplicates the existing owner. |
| Unslop | **Keep one anti-pattern owner under Writing for Humans; assess Unslop as source replacement for Stop Slop, rather than an additional mandatory pass.** The paper fit is better on preserving meaning, passive exceptions and natural group size. Adoption should record a specific conflict ruling and retain necessary uncertainty/domain names. Output quality still needs a small trial. | Until replacement is chosen, make Unslop an explicitly selected alternative catalog inside the same writing package. A selective merge can add stable IDs, vague-attribution and over-compression checks, but must remove redundant/conflicting rules rather than layer both catalogs. | A new default native Unslop plus mandatory Stop Slop makes users choose the package instead of the writing outcome and invites sequential conflicting passes. Replacing Writing for Humans itself with either catalog discards its broader contract. |

This composition does not require renaming either upstream source: source identity/pin remains independent even when its user-facing entry is Writing for Humans. Technical Writing's Unslop call should resolve to its bundled method or an explicit chosen-catalog ruling, not to an assumed AFK installation. Source replacement would retain old provenance/receipt history and record the new carried source, rather than silently mutate Stop Slop. These are packaging recommendations supported by Atlas's existing source-composition model. [Atlas maintenance/distribution](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/docs/authoring/source-composition.md), [current Writing methods and references](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/sources/composition.json#L1507-L1536).

**No Comments source contract versus router contracts.** No Comments orders comment deletion/refactor execution, ambiguity toward deletion, accepted findings implemented, Architect for nontrivial fixes, and deletion even after an encoding offer is declined. Comment Sicko protects externally forced surprises and some public contracts but kills internal surprises and flags their symbols for redesign. Atlas Simplify promises exact behavior preservation, project conventions and understanding before deletion; its carried method already deletes “what” narration and preserves “why” comments. Atlas Review explicitly remains read-only, already assesses standards/spec, and allows construction references only as proposed changes. Its ownership document is a concrete existing precedent for adapting execution-oriented source guidance to review. [No Comments L19-24](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/no-comments/SKILL.md#L19-L24), [Comment Sicko L14-30](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/no-comments/references/comment-sicko.md#L14-L30), [Simplify router](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-simplify/SKILL.md), [Simplify preservation L30-59 and comment rules L137-145](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-simplify/references/simplification/code-simplification.md#L30-L145), [Review router L9-23](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-review/SKILL.md#L9-L23), [Review ownership L5-9](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-review/references/ownership.md#L5-L9).

**No Comments topology judgment.**

- **Preferred now: skip the full workflow.** Existing Simplify already captures the low-risk deletion value, and Code Review can flag unjustified suppression/workaround comments against project standards. Fresh independence is already part of Code review. The special deletion-on-doubt policy is the part most incompatible with Atlas; removing it leaves a much smaller increment than the original note implied. This is an adoption judgment, not a claim composition is impossible. [Code review independent axes](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-review/references/code/code-review.md#L7-L12), [documented standards override smell baselines L69-79](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-review/references/code/code-review.md#L69-L79).
- **Plausible only with demonstrated need:** add a requested Comment Audit specialization under Atlas Review, carrying a pinned, patched Comment Sicko prompt. It reports exact symbol/evidence, justified keeps, suppression validity and potential enforcement, with zero deletions. Existing Code Standards could consume this specialist when comment/suppression scrutiny is requested; a top-level Comments mode earns its place only if users need a narrow audit without the full two-axis Code review. This requires substantive semantic adaptation, not just host syntax.
- **Plausible implementation follow-through:** Atlas Simplify can consume accepted audit findings for behavior-preserving removal/rename/extraction, preserving invariant/contract/edge-case/trade-off comments and leaving ambiguous constraints intact. Architecture or behavior changes go to an authorized executor; no automatic Architect/Arena expansion for ordinary cleanup. If adopted this is a composed technique, not a faithful execution of No Comments.
- **Rejected:** automatic No Comments before every review, a native unchanged No Comments default, or replacement of Simplify with it. Native packaging does not fix the semantic conflict. A new Atlas Comments package would multiply entry points without resolving it.

### Inferences

The practical distinction is outcome ownership, not how many upstream repositories exist. Writing already has an owner for both technical-doc and anti-pattern methods. Review can host a comment audit, but present overlap and conflict make “possible composition” weaker than “recommended adoption.”

### Gaps

No trial demonstrates that a patched Comment Audit catches material defects the current reviewers miss, or that Unslop outperforms the existing writing composition. Those are adoption tests, not reasons to default to standalone packages.

## Where should audit trails, corrective learning, and personalization live?

### Takeaway

Show Me Your Work's best integration point is an optional audit method in Tracking. Correct and Retro form a coherent diagnosis-to-enforcement composition, but that is a separate choice from adopting Correct at all. Automate Me does not fit an existing execution/review router well enough to justify forcing it there.

### Cited Findings

**Audit source contracts.** Show Me Your Work owns an append-only TSV with evidence/result, run ownership, transcript reconciliation and mandatory different-model-family review, and mandates an Attention section in every reply for a run producing a trail. Tracking already owns a single implementation record/home, material decisions, evidence, complete review resolution and explicit acceptance; secondary representations require a synchronization agreement. HumanLayer Show Me only guides visual presentation; Plannotator provides handoff/acceptance. [audit method L9-82](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/show-me-your-work/SKILL.md#L9-L82), [Tracking home L19-38](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/tracking-implementation/SKILL.md#L19-L38), [Tracking gate/content L112-138](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/tracking-implementation/SKILL.md#L112-L138).

**Why Retro+Correct composition is substantive rather than invented grouping.** Matt Retro explicitly diagnoses environment improvements from a session and presents candidates; its categories include existing guardrail wiring, deterministic checks, instruction bloat and tools. Correct looks for classes recurring twice, prioritizes architecture/types/checks over prose, implements repairs and proves guards against real past mistakes. They share the same long-lived problem—improving the agent's environment—but have different action contracts. A Diagnose/Enforce router resolves that difference, rather than grouping them because both “learn.” A composed maintained `retro` entry can keep a familiar name; an Atlas prefix is optional. The routing cost is justified only if Atlas wants one learning entry point. Otherwise two explicit commands remain reasonable, and evidence does not force either choice. [Retro L7-25](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/engineering/retro/SKILL.md#L7-L25), [Correct L9-32](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/correct/SKILL.md#L9-L32), [current Implement methods](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/skills/atlas-implement/SKILL.md#L11-L20).

**Required rulings for those integrations.** Tracking must explicitly decide whether “one required artifact” has an opted-in audit attachment and whether the Attention footer is retained per handoff or every response; silently ignoring that source requirement is not verbatim adoption. Different-family review/transcript access need host capability handling. Retro Enforce must not auto-trigger on every correction, must preserve authorized scope/commit policy, and must use Writing for Agents for rule-file changes. Automate Me requires current-workspace transcript boundaries, approval of inferred durable preferences, no copying Poteto personal autonomy policy, and replacing unconditional worktree/commit/PR with authorized delivery. Its manual invocation and explicit every-turn opt-in are useful upstream protections to keep. [audit L42-74](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/show-me-your-work/SKILL.md#L42-L74), [Correct rule writes/commits L26-30](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/correct/SKILL.md#L26-L30), [Automate Me scope/confirmation L29-56](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/automate-me/SKILL.md#L29-L56), [manual activation/iteration/landing L75-104](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/automate-me/SKILL.md#L75-L104).

### Inferences

- Preferred order is to decide the capability and owner first, then choose bundled reference versus exposed command. Dependency independence says that Correct is cheap to carry; it does not establish that users need a separate package.
- For Tracking, the ledger is a complementary evidence method, not another tracking system. The exact TSV may remain an attachment because rewriting it into mutable record prose would discard its append-only and transcript-audit properties.
- For Retro, composition is a defensible option with a concrete safety/action boundary. It is not an existing router at this pin and must be presented as proposed package work rather than already supported.

### Gaps

The user has not chosen to introduce a maintained Retro composition or to require audit trails. These are transparent topology alternatives. No model/host trial yet establishes that their benefits outweigh added maintenance and review cost.

## Which Bro variants should stay independent, and what topology is actually recommended?

### Takeaway

Bro's four existing commands are already tiny, explicit conversational operations with distinct human intent. Folding them into a broad router has little demonstrated benefit. Preserve the existing selections and do not duplicate Pstack Bro; missing redistribution evidence remains a bundling constraint rather than a reason to invent wrappers.

### Cited Findings

Pstack Bro, upstream Bro and installed Bro were verified byte-identical in the earlier research, while installed Facts, Readback and Recap also match the same Bro pin. Atlas's stack already selects all four. They have no transitive workflow/tool dependencies and all explicitly disable implicit invocation in frontmatter and host metadata. Bro's tracked tree/manifests at this pin do not declare a license; Pstack separately declares MIT. Existing source selection does not establish permission for Atlas vendoring. [Pstack Bro](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/skills/bro/SKILL.md), [Bro source tree](https://github.com/backnotprop/bro/tree/523ed36584ec877b975db8c418ef12d485235d0e/skills), [Atlas selections L76-86](https://github.com/logbookfordevs/logbook-atlas/blob/1d671b4cb4d5193891f5f98d9cfdd726741a0c6d/stacks/atlas.json#L76-L86), [Bro manifest](https://github.com/backnotprop/bro/blob/523ed36584ec877b975db8c418ef12d485235d0e/.codex-plugin/plugin.json), [Bro tracked tree](https://github.com/backnotprop/bro/tree/523ed36584ec877b975db8c418ef12d485235d0e), [Pstack MIT](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/1f0f9c3bac03c4297f6fc8f7644fa412eda57085/LICENSE).

### Inferences

**Proposed topology, in priority order.**

1. **Writing for Humans owns technical-document method plus one anti-pattern source.** Technical Writing is a strong conditional branch candidate; Unslop is a source-replacement/alternative decision within that same package. No new writing entry point is needed.
2. **Tracking optionally owns decision auditing.** Show Me Your Work is a candidate attached evidence method with source-policy rulings, not mandatory ceremony and not a substitute for Show Me, review, QA or Plannotator acceptance.
3. **If a consolidated learning workflow is desired, compose Retro Diagnose/Enforce with Correct.** If consolidation is not desired, retain Retro and consider native Correct. Both are legitimate; source independence does not decide between them. Existing Implement can execute the selected repairs without becoming the retrospective-discovery owner.
4. **Do not adopt full No Comments now.** Review/Simplify are feasible homes for a narrowly patched technique, but distinct incremental value must justify discarding much of upstream's destructive policy. This is a weaker adoption candidate than the writing/audit integrations.
5. **Defer Automate Me's default adoption; keep a separate explicit personalization workflow if requested.** No existing router has a sufficiently close outcome contract, and creating a general meta-router would be premature.
6. **Keep already-used Bro variants without duplication.** They are a defensible independent-command case because each is direct, manual and conversational, not because upstream keeps separate skill folders.

Required packaged support and host limitations remain those documented in the earlier note: Technical Writing includes Unslop unless a deliberate catalog substitution ruling replaces that call; the audit includes TSV/header/helper and transcript/cross-family capability; a faithful No Comments would pull How/Why/Architect/Arena and principle references, while a deliberately scoped review technique should not pretend to retain that execution closure. Composition may reduce the active dependency path, but only through an explicit behavior adaptation with provenance, not by dropping inconvenient calls. ### Gaps

No runtime packages, source snapshots or manifests were changed. These findings do not turn a source contract into demonstrated model behavior, nor establish missing Bro redistribution permission. PE Verify and Interrogate placement are outside this assigned slice; no conclusion here prevents source-composing their independently maintained methods into an appropriate existing router.
