How It Works

  1. Shower thought. Ask in chat, or say "open the desk" and let it interview you. Every room in the Desk is optional and nothing is written by visiting, which means you can always just tell the agent in chat what you want and skip the whole interface if you prefer that.
  2. brief.md. Every piece has one, and its first heading is the piece's title.
  3. The Desk. Pick a vibe, gather references, plan beats against a track.
  4. Skill time. The right skill takes over and runs its own process.
  5. Render. Frames come out of code into renders/.
  6. QA. Evidence goes in qa/.
  7. Ship it? Only if Leonardo says so.
"The Desk never runs a skill or a model itself. It writes plain files beside the piece, and agents do the work."
