How the room works

  • Rendered from code

    drawFrame(ctx, i) is the camera. Every frame comes out of code, so a render can always be rebuilt.

  • A Desk, if you want one

    Pick a vibe, gather references and plan beats against a track. Every room is optional, and chat is always a complete alternative.

  • 46 skills on call

    Coworkers who never take lunch, kept in their upstream form. Once a piece has a brief, the right one takes over.

How a piece is born

Seven stops from a shower thought to a final. Leonardo’s brief beats every skill’s defaults along the way.

  1. 01

    Shower thought

    Ask in chat, or say “open the desk” and let it interview you.

  2. 02

    brief.md

    Every piece has one, and its first heading is the piece’s title.

  3. 03

    The Desk

    Pick a vibe, gather references and plan beats against a track.

  4. 04

    Skill time

    The right skill takes over and runs its own process.

  5. 05

    Render

    Frames come out of code into renders/, which can always be rebuilt.

  6. 06

    QA

    Evidence goes in qa/. At checkpoints, Leonardo judges an interactive review page.

  7. 07

    Ship it?

    Only if Leonardo says so. Approved finals live in the piece’s final/ folder.

The Desk

A live local studio in your browser: an interview, a Lobby of pieces and five rooms per piece.

Overview
The piece at a glance: hand-off, renders, checkpoint feedback and files.
Board
A reference canvas for looks, stills and tablet sketches.
Beats
A timing strip with the song, the beats and a sheet agents can read.
Asks
Requests to agents and their answers, waiting as files until someone listens.
Review
A frame-accurate render with notes and measured facts.

Say hello to the Desk

Needs Node 22.12 or later and pnpm 11. Run these from the repository root.

  1. pnpm install Installs every dependency. Let pnpm manage the links.
  2. pnpm desk:start Finds or starts this checkout’s Desk and prints its URL.
  3. pnpm studio:doctor Checks your tools. ffmpeg-full, anyone?

In Claude Code, /desk does the same and opens it. Run pnpm lint before you leave.
