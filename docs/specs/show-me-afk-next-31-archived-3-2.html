DIRECTION FOR REVIEW

Your skills.
A continuous maintenance path.

Independent operations by default. Compose when the resulting operation earns its place. Keep selected upstream skills under one roof, with reviewed updates.

5 October 2026 · Revises the umbrella-first direction. Recommendations below are not accepted migrations. Current Skills V2 work remains an experiment; no installed skills change.

Revisit the twelve candidates

Decisions so far: Investigate and Plan removed; Decide manual and Design automatic retained. Other candidates remain under review. Historical sections preserve every previous umbrella.

What Atlas owns now

Your authored workflows, maintained forks, selected unchanged upstream skills, provenance and scoped conflict decisions. Authored methods become the umbrella’s own implementation; legacy copies are historical only. Source composition bundles upstream methods and resources. Behavior composition uses independent skills resolved through the host, including any called by a bundled method. AFK handles availability and profiles; Skills CLI handles installation. A profile is a grouping, not a conflict-resolution process.

What survives from our earlier work

Source snapshots, hashes, license receipts, canonical inputs, generated portable outputs, conditional loading and meaningful conflict rulings. PE Design, Build, Review and Verify contributions remain accepted in principle, with concrete source selection pending. Coordinate is deferred; writing, Handoff, tools, Impeccable, Architect and Clean Room remain independent.

One roof. Explicit provenance.

Moving from unchanged to adapted keeps one authoring and update path. Atlas distributes the revision it reviewed, which may lag upstream.

Every source has its receipt

Original author, repository, path, immutable commit, license/notices, source and carried hashes, classification and consumers. A composed skill can carry sources with different classifications.

Updates stay deliberate

Deduplicate candidate proposals. Flag affected patches, rulings, dependencies and consumers. Keep the accepted pin until review; failed access never means up to date. No silent merge or installed-file mutation.

Useful adaptations survive

Existing Matt forks retain their intentional changes. New Atlas behavior belongs in authored instructions; patch upstream for a documented conflict. A caller override also counts as an adaptation.

Maintenance has a boundary

Carry skills actively used or intentionally maintained. Preserve attribution and redistribution rights. Choose one installed identity per operation to avoid competing upstream and Atlas copies.

Composition follows the useful operation.

Spec and tickets: separate first

Keep the two intentional forks independently invocable and synchronized. A future Plan skill can behaviorally compose a useful sequence: spec → human review → tickets. Shared topic alone does not require absorption.

Two motion approaches: composition candidate

One authored motion contract can use both sources. Preserve each reference verbatim where possible, patch only necessary conflicts, and own the selection rulings. An upstream change flags the affected ruling for review.

Bro and a new explanation method

Start as siblings. If one clear clarification operation is more useful, compose selected references later. Keep direct entry points where they still earn their place; distinguish triggers to avoid competing automatic behavior.

Research: independent operation

Investigate was removed. Research remains automatic, independent and verbatim. A scoped umbrella needs a useful routing or reconciliation purpose rather than a one-source wrapper.

Next review

Confirm the independently valuable operations and callers. Formalize fork receipts, select the actively maintained upstream set, then build reviewed update proposals. Try useful compositions individually. The active roster, naming, watcher cadence and migration remain open.
