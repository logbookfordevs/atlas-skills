Every source has its receipt

Original author, repository, path, immutable commit, license/notices, source and carried hashes, classification and consumers. A composed skill can carry sources with different classifications.

Updates stay deliberate

Deduplicate candidate proposals. Flag affected patches, rulings, dependencies and consumers. Keep the accepted pin until review; failed access never means up to date. No silent merge or installed-file mutation.

Useful adaptations survive

Existing Matt forks retain their intentional changes. New Atlas behavior belongs in authored instructions; patch upstream for a documented conflict. A caller override also counts as an adaptation.

Maintenance has a boundary

Carry skills actively used or intentionally maintained. Preserve attribution and redistribution rights. Choose one installed identity per operation to avoid competing upstream and Atlas copies.

Composition follows the useful operation.

Specify: preserve both forks

Specify composes the existing To Spec and To Tickets verbatim patched forks. Preserve their intentional adaptations and separate upstream records. Exact modes and public naming remain to be agreed.

Implementation: executor plus tracking

Implement Spec and Source Driven Development remain upstream skills. Tracking Implementation activates for requested tracking and records the selected executor’s progress, evidence, acceptance and recovery.

Clarify: deferred

Bro, Facts, Readback, Recap and Teach remain independent. No Clarify umbrella is being built; Show Me also remains independent.

Research: independent operation

Investigate was removed. Research remains automatic, independent and verbatim. A scoped umbrella needs a useful routing or reconciliation purpose rather than a one-source wrapper.

Next review

Confirm the independently valuable operations and callers. Formalize fork receipts, select the actively maintained upstream set, then build reviewed update proposals. Try useful compositions individually. The active roster, naming, watcher cadence and migration remain open.
